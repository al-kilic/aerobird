How grades work

Grading rules

A≥ 120 effective survey units. Shown normally, ±30% interval.
B≥ 60 units. Shown, ±55% interval.
C≥ 25 units. Indicative only, ±90% interval.
NoneBelow 25. No score is published. Never shown as low.

Units: one per complete checklist, two per airside log entry, thirty when radar covered the week. Scaled by how much of each source falls in the selected time of day.

Risk = κ · Σ hazard × severity × exposure. Units: expected damaging strikes per 100,000 movements. Hazard is effort-normalised and zero-filled; severity is a logistic fit on log body mass; exposure is the share of each ring flown below the bird layer.
